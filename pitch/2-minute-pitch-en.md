# 2-Minute Pitch Script - Sinergy Sol

## Title: The Gap Between What Companies Pay and What Workers Receive

### Main Version

A few years ago, I worked for a U.S. company that hired talent in Colombia through another company.

In the office, we were doing the work. We had the schedules, the deliverables, the client pressure, the responsibility. But one day, I learned from a reliable source that in some cases, the money being sent from the U.S. for those roles was more than double what we were actually receiving.

And that stayed with me.

Because the problem was not that the company did not value the talent. The problem was that between the company and the people doing the work, there was an expensive, opaque, hard-to-audit middle layer. A layer capturing a huge part of the value just for standing in the middle.

That story is not unique to me. It is part of the reality for thousands of professionals across Latin America.

Today, global companies want to hire talent in LatAm, but paying those teams is still slow, expensive, and unclear. Many rely on outsourcing firms, EORs, payroll intermediaries, or fragmented manual processes. The result is painful: companies pay more, talent receives less, and nobody has full visibility into where the money is being lost.

In other words, companies are not just paying salaries. They are paying for complexity.

Sinergy Sol was created to close that gap.

We are payment infrastructure that helps global companies pay talent in Latin America in a more direct, faster, and transparent way.

With Sinergy Sol, a company can register beneficiaries, upload payment batches, review a clear quote with fees and FX, approve the payout, fund in USDC, and execute local payments to workers in countries like Colombia and Mexico.

But we are not just another app for moving money. We are an operating console for finance teams, with approvals, reconciliation, traceability, audit logs, and exception handling when something fails.

The difference is simple: fewer intermediaries, less opacity, and more control over every dollar sent and every payment received.

I experienced this gap from the worker's side. Sinergy Sol solves it from the infrastructure side.

Because Latin American talent does not need more layers between their work and their pay. They need modern, transparent, and fair rails to receive the value they are already creating.

Sinergy Sol closes the gap between global companies and Latin America's talent.
