# Delivery Quick Reference Card

## Word Count & Timing

| Section | Approx. Words | Time |
|---------|---------------|------|
| Hook (Personal Story) | ~65 | 0:00–0:20 |
| Problem (Market Pain) | ~85 | 0:20–0:50 |
| Solution (Sinergy Sol) | ~110 | 0:50–1:30 |
| Close (Emotional Return) | ~75 | 1:30–2:00 |
| **Total** | **~335** | **2:00** |

> **Note:** 335 words is slightly over the typical 250-280 word target for 2 minutes. Practice reading aloud at a measured pace with pauses. If you need to trim, the Solution section can be shortened by removing one bullet point from the workflow description.

## Key Numbers to Remember

- **2x** — salary gap (US pay vs. Colombia pay)
- **3** — intermediaries removed (EOR, outsourcing, manual processes)
- **4** — core features (beneficiaries, batches, approvals, reconciliation)
- **2** — initial corridors (Colombia, Mexico)
- **1** — funding currency (USDC on Solana)

## Transition Phrases

| From | To | Bridge Phrase |
|------|-----|---------------|
| Hook | Problem | "That story is not unique to me." |
| Problem | Solution | "Sinergy Sol is infrastructure built for this exact problem." |
| Solution | Close | "That gap I mentioned at the beginning..." |

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Rushing the hook** — The personal story is your differentiator. Slow down here.
2. **Over-explaining the tech** — Judges care about the problem first. USDC/Solana are details, not the headline.
3. **Losing the emotional thread** — Always circle back to the human impact.
4. **Going over 2 minutes** — Practice with a timer. The close is the easiest section to trim if needed.

## Practice Routine

1. **Read aloud once** — Focus on flow, not timing
2. **Read with timer** — Aim for 1:50 to allow for natural pauses
3. **Record yourself** — Listen for pace, emphasis, and clarity
4. **Practice without reading** — Know the structure, not just the words
